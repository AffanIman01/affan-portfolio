OFFICIAL THEME / PRACTICE MODEL

The Pursuit of
Mustika Nusantara.

Collect. Transfer. Build. Enshrine.
Two specialized robots, one three-minute mission.

01

Collect & transfer

TR gathers Earth and Sky blocks, then climbs the ramp to release them inside the transfer area.

02

Build two towers

BR stacks two Earth blocks and a Sky cap per tower. Include a shared-area tower to unlock the Sanctuary Mandate.

03

Enshrine the Mustika

TR retrieves the unlocked ball. BR carries it to the central L2 pillar. The match continues to 180 seconds.

Source & model boundaries

Based on the organizer-linked rulebook V1.0, checked 1 October 2026. Field offsets are reconstructed from diagrams. Robot designs are original proposals.

This is solo, assisted handling practice. Base-footprint zone guards are simplified. No opponent AI, tower stealing, contact dynamics, friction, tipping, sensor noise or referee adjudication. Object release snaps to legal locations. Routes and timings are idealized; they are not hardware performance predictions. The builder uses a scripted state machine with perfect object detection. The practice reset returns TR's held objects to supply.
